GeoNames data attribution

Awqati includes a transformed subset of the GeoNames geographical database.
The included set is derived from GeoNames cities500, with Arabic and English
alternate names and administrative reference data from the official GeoNames
dump dated 2026-09-11.

The generated metadata.json records the exact official source URLs and SHA-256
digests. Raw GeoNames source files are build inputs only and are not distributed
inside the NVDA add-on.

Arabic priority-country names supplement (build-time only)

Wikidata contributors: Arabic labels linked by GeoNames ID (P1566), retrieved
2026-09-15 from https://query.wikidata.org/sparql in complete bounded queries.
Wikidata structured data is available under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication):
https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The versioned extraction and query/response hashes are in the source repository
(data_sources/wikidata_arabic_names.v1.json); no network dependency is added.

Reviewed Saudi and UAE place names are geographic name facts, not reproduced
articles or maps. Source URLs, review dates and GeoNames identities are recorded
in data_sources/sa_locations_supplement.v1.json and
 data_sources/arabic_reviewed_names.v1.json. Saudi official authority:
https://ncar.gov.sa/regions-coding. UAE source authorities include DEWA, KHDA,
RTA, Dubai Statistics, Abu Dhabi municipality and Sharjah Government Media Bureau.
Generic GeoNames administrative labels Sector N are displayed in Arabic as
القطاع N through the versioned build policy. Unknown administrative codes are
omitted from human labels. GeoNames coordinates retain their CC BY 4.0 license.
